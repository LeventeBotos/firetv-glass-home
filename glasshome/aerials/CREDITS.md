# Aerial footage

The screensaver plays free stock footage from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com), used under the
[Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) (free to use, no attribution required; credit is appreciated).
The clips are not part of this repository: `fetch.py` downloads them from Pexels, and the page for each clip is
listed in `clips.txt`. Apple's own Aerial videos are Apple's, so they are not used.

Clips and their pages: see `clips.txt`. Shots include coastal cliffs in Portugal and elsewhere, desert dunes,
an alpine valley, a waterfall in a forested gorge and a turquoise cove.
